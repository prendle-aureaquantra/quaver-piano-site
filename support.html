What Quaver does

Quaver: Learn Piano is an iPad app that helps children learn to play a real piano. Notes fall onto an on-screen keyboard while Quaver, a friendly purple music note, listens through the iPad’s microphone.

  • Practice mode waits for your child to play each note before moving on.
  • Watch mode plays the piece so they can hear how it goes.
  • Learn the right hand, the left hand or both – the app can play the other hand.
  • Adjustable speed, rainbow note colours, and long pieces split into short parts.
  • Earn up to 3 stars per song, keep a streak going, and hear Quaver cheer.
  • 44+ songs: warm-up tunes, classical favourites, Mozart, Chopin & more, and Scott Joplin ragtime – with more being added.
  • The composer: describe an idea, such as “a happy song about my cat”, and Quaver writes a short original piece to learn.

Getting started

  1. Put the iPad on the music stand of your piano, in landscape.
  2. Allow the microphone when Quaver asks, so it can hear the piano. Sound is analysed on the iPad and never recorded or sent anywhere.
  3. Choose a song. The warm-up tunes are a lovely place to begin.
  4. Play along in Practice mode. There’s no rush – Quaver waits for you to play each note.

If you said no to the microphone by mistake, turn it back on in the iPad’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

If Quaver doesn’t hear your notes

  • Find a quiet room. TVs, chatter and other music can confuse the listening.
  • Keep the iPad close to the piano – on the music stand is ideal.
  • Slow the tempo down so there’s time for each note to be heard.
  • Play a little firmer. Very soft notes can be hard to pick up.

Subscriptions

Quaver is free to download and includes the warm-up tunes and a selection of classical pieces. Quaver Plus (monthly or yearly, with a free trial) unlocks every song, all future songs and the composer. It works with Family Sharing.

Manage or cancel

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To change or cancel, open Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPad (tap your name at the top of Settings) and choose Quaver Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Restore purchases

New iPad, or reinstalled the app? Open the Parents’ corner in Quaver and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with (or one in the same Family Sharing group).

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with a digital piano or keyboard?

Yes. Play it through its speakers (not headphones) so the iPad can hear it.

Which iPads are supported?

Any iPad running iPadOS 17 or later. The AI composer needs an iPad that supports Apple Intelligence; other iPads use Quaver’s built-in composer instead, so every child can still write songs.

Does Quaver record my child?

No. Microphone sound is analysed live on the iPad to detect notes and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. See our Privacy Policy.

Do we need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Progress and composed songs stay on the iPad.

Where does the music come from?

All songs are public domain or freely licensed. Full credits are in the app.
