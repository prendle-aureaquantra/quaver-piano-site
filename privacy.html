Quaver: Learn Piano

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

The short version: Quaver: Learn Piano collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Everything your child does in the app stays on your iPad.

Who we are

Quaver: Learn Piano (“Quaver”, “the app”) is an iPad app that helps children learn to play the piano. It is made by Paul Rendleman (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, email info@aureaquantra.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you or your child. The app has:

  • no accounts or sign-in;
  • no advertising;
  • no analytics or usage tracking;
  • no third-party SDKs;
  • no tracking across other apps or websites.

The microphone

Quaver asks for microphone access so it can hear your piano. The sound is analysed live, on the iPad, only to work out which notes are being played. Microphone audio is never recorded, never stored and never sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access on or off at any time in the iPad’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

What stays on the iPad

A few things are saved so the app can remember where your child is up to. They are stored only on the device and are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Progress – stars earned and practice streaks.
  • First name (optional) – if your child types a first name, it is used only so Quaver can greet them.
  • Composed songs – songs your child creates with the composer.

Deleting the app removes this information. If your iPad is backed up to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include the app’s data in that backup under Apple’s own privacy terms; we cannot see those backups.

The AI composer

The composer turns your child’s idea (for example “a happy song about my cat”) into a short piece of music. On iPads that support Apple Intelligence it uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models; on other iPads it uses a composer built into the app. In both cases everything happens on the iPad. What your child types and the music that is created are not sent off the device.

Purchases

Quaver Plus subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, payment card or other billing details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active so it can unlock the right songs. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check so children cannot buy things by accident.

Children’s privacy

Quaver is designed for children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the relevant age in your country). We believe this meets the requirements of the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the children’s data provisions of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as there is no personal data for us to process, share or sell.

Sharing and selling data

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not have any.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to return or delete; the information described above lives on your iPad and is removed when you delete the app. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change ever meant collecting personal information, we would ask for consent first where the law requires it.
